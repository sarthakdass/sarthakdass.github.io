> *Every single statistic is a made-up metric, but they help us make sense of and compare things.*
>
> *- legendary baseball statistician Bill James*

Yesterday, on December 4, 2025, LeBron James finished the game having scored 8 points on 4-17 shooting. The last time LeBron James scored less than 10 points in a regular season game, I was 6 years old. Now, I'm applying for my master's degree.

I was helping one of my friends move yesterday so I did unfortunately miss the game but when I first saw the stat line, I remember feeling something that I didn't know how to express. LeBron James has basically been playing basketball for every year that I actively remember, and this is the first year where I can see signs of him aging and slowing down on the basketball court, no matter what he says. No longer is he a clear top 10 player in the league (in my opinion), and his value is far more limited than what he used to provide. For once, I can definitively say that the odds have tipped in favor of Father Time.

The NBA has a rookie sensation by the name of Cooper Flagg. Flagg was so good as a high schooler that he was invited to play on the practice squad against the USA Olympic team to help them prepare for the Olympics. Cooper Flagg intentionally reclassified (skipped a grade, essentially) just so he could get to college and the NBA quickly.

Cooper Flagg was born on **December 21, 2006.**

The last time LeBron James scored less than 10 points was **January 5, 2007.**

With Chris Paul recently getting kicked off the Clippers (rather disrespectfully), the active player who has played the most number of games is Russell Westbrook.

Russell Westbrook has played **1,259 games in his entire career.**

LeBron James' 10+ scoring streak lasted **1,297 consecutive games**.

The beginning of LeBron's streak is older than the iPhone.

Here are the following current active streaks of scoring 10+ points in a game:

- Kevin Durant – 267 (Apr. 25, 2021).
- Shai Gilgeous-Alexander – 170 (Oct. 30, 2023).
- Luka Doncic – 157 (Jan. 30, 2023).
- Nikola Jokic – 133 (Jan. 10, 2024).
- Devin Booker – 120 (Feb. 22, 2024).
- Jayson Tatum – 111 (Jan. 13, 2024).
- Cade Cunningham – 101 (Mar. 5, 2024).
- Franz Wagner – 77 (Nov. 1, 2024).
- Jalen Williams – 72 (Oct. 24, 2024).
- James Harden – 53 (Feb. 6, 2025).

*On a rather unfortunate note, Kevin Durant unfairly got his streak snapped short as in the middle of the game, his COVID test result determined to be positive so he was removed from the game. Aside from this one game, his streak would be at 829 consecutive games.*

Kevin Durant is 37 years old and will likely not be the one to usurp LeBron's record.

Shai Gilgeous-Alexander is currently one of the best, if not the best scorer in the league currently and he is 27 years old. If Shai Gilgeous-Alexander plays every game for the next 14 years, he can surpass this record at the ripe age of 40, barring no untimely injuries. The sheer notion of this separation between LeBron and the rest of the league is ridiculously goofy and absurd.

And finally, if you collectively add all of these 10 closest candidates' streaks up, **they total to 1261 games**. Again, LeBron James' streak ended at 1297.

So, what exactly happened on December 4th, 2025? How did LeBron James fail?

Having a rough shooting night, LeBron was 4-17 from the floor, being guarded by the especially versatile defender Scottie Barnes. But despite LeBron's poor scoring, the Lakers and Raptors were tied 120-120, in large part due to his teammate Austin Reaves who was having a monster game with 44 points.

With 5 seconds left, LeBron's defender leaves him to double team Austin Reaves. Reaves passes the ball to LeBron James, giving him a chance to score two points to win the game and keep his streak alive. Here's how it unfolds.

LeBron James made the game-winning pass for his 11th assist of the game.

Just like he's done time and time again throughout his career, LeBron James makes the right play and finds his open teammate, the good shooter Rui Hachimura, wide open in the corner who buries the 3-pointer at the buzzer to win the game. I think it's poetic that even on the day where LeBron James loses one of the most mind-boggling statistical streaks of all time, he does it with a game-winning assist.
