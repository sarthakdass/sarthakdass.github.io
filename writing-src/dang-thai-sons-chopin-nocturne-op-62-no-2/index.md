This is one of my favorite performances of all time: it's so tragically beautiful. Dang Thai Son grew up during the Vietnam War, so he learned piano while seeking refuge in a remote mountain village, by lining up planks on his bed to form some sort of a run-down piano. He didn't own a proper suit for the Chopin Competition, so the Polish government had to help him get a suit two days before the final round in order to participate because it was nationally televised.

In the final round, the requirement was to perform with an orchestra (not linked here) and this was his first time ever performing with an orchestra. He killed it and won the competition in 1980, becoming the first Asian to do so and has gone on to teach so many great pianists! Also, I have met him once and he is very funny.
